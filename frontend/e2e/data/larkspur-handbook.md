# Larkspur Observatory maintenance handbook

This handbook is a test fixture for the end-to-end suite. Every fact in it is invented.

## Primary mirror

The Larkspur Observatory primary mirror is recoated with aluminium every 47 days.
Recoating takes place in the north dome clean room and is supervised by the optics lead.

## Dome shutters

The dome shutters are lubricated at the start of each observing season, before first light.
A shutter inspection checklist is kept in the control room logbook.

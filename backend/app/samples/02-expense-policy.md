# Northwind Traders — Travel & Expense Policy (sample)

> Sample content shipped with the platform. Northwind Traders is a fictional company.

## Approval thresholds

- Expenses up to 500 EUR: line manager approval.
- Expenses from 500 to 5,000 EUR: department head approval.
- Expenses above 5,000 EUR: CFO approval.

## Travel

Economy class is required for flights under six hours. Hotel costs are reimbursed up to 180 EUR per night
in capital cities and 140 EUR elsewhere.

## Meals

Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to 55 EUR per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Submitting claims

Submit claims with receipts within 30 days through the finance portal. Late claims need CFO sign-off.

# Northwind Traders — Employee Handbook (sample)

> Sample content shipped with the platform. Northwind Traders is a fictional company.

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00–15:00 local time. Teams agree their remaining hours with their manager.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 26 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays.
Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next year and must be used by 31 March.

## Sick leave

Employees may take up to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is needed for
absences longer than three consecutive working days.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements need director approval.

## Equipment

New starters receive a laptop on their first day. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.
